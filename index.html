About me

Solutions organizations adopt with confidence.

I'm a results-driven Salesforce Administrator and Business Analyst with over five years of experience delivering scalable CRM solutions that improve operational efficiency, optimize business processes, and enhance data quality. As a five-time Salesforce-certified professional, I specialize in transforming complex business requirements into intuitive, user-centered Salesforce solutions through configuration, Flow automation, security, custom objects, validation rules, reports, dashboards, and process optimization.

My approach combines technical expertise with strategic business analysis to design solutions organizations can adopt with confidence. I enjoy collaborating with stakeholders, analyzing business needs, and implementing Salesforce solutions that deliver measurable outcomes and support long-term organizational growth.

To strengthen my ability to solve complex business and technology challenges, I earned an M.S. in Applied Information Technology and a Graduate Certificate in Health Information Technology from Towson University. These programs broadened my expertise in data management, systems integration, analytics, and emerging technologies, complementing my Salesforce experience and preparing me to deliver solutions across multiple industries.

Beyond my professional work, I'm passionate about sharing knowledge and advancing the Salesforce community through TalentsPoint, where I create practical tutorials on Salesforce, automation, artificial intelligence, and digital technologies. Teaching and mentoring let me simplify complex concepts and help others build successful technology careers.

As technology continues to evolve, I'm expanding my expertise in Salesforce Agentforce, Data Cloud, and AI-powered CRM solutions. My goal is to help organizations harness intelligent automation, connected data, and innovative Salesforce technologies to improve decision-making, increase productivity, and deliver exceptional customer experiences.
